02 / Business setup

We help new business owners through the same path we took to set up Weisone. We went through every one of these steps for Weisone ourselves, and we'll walk you through them.

  • Forming the limited liability company (LLC)
  • Getting an Employer Identification Number (EIN)
  • Creating a Login.gov account
  • Getting a Unique Entity ID (UEI) and registering on SAM.gov
  • Getting a CAGE code
  • Opening a business bank account
  • Finding grant and funding programs, including SBIR and NSF programs, and Small Business Development Center (SBDC) programs

SAM.gov registration is free from the government. Weisone is not a government agency, a law firm, or an accountant. This is guidance, not legal or tax advice.
